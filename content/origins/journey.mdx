# The Journey

Every engineer has an origin story. Mine began not with a grand vision, but with
curiosity — a simple question about how things work, followed by the discovery
that I could build them myself.

## The Beginning

The first program was nothing special. A few lines of C that printed text to a
screen. But it was the first time I felt the power of creation through code.

The machine did exactly what I told it to do. No ambiguity. No interpretation.
Pure logic.

## The Path

From there, the path was set. Every late night debugging, every breakthrough
moment, every failed project — they all led here.

To someone who builds not because they have to, but because they cannot imagine
doing anything else.
