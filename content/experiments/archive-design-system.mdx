# Archive Design System

Exploring a cohesive design language for digital archives.

## Inspiration

- Physical books and museum catalogs
- Editorial magazines
- Apple's design philosophy
- A24 film aesthetics

## Key Elements

- Paper textures and archival colors
- Editorial typography with strong hierarchy
- Cinematic interactions and transitions
- Generous whitespace
- Intentional motion design

## Notes

The goal is to make digital feel tangible. Every interaction should feel like
turning a page in a well-crafted book.
